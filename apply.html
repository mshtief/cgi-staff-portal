Thank you for your interest in spending your summer with us at Camp Gan Israel Morristown!

Working at Camp Gan Israel is a balance between being the most fun person in the world while maintaining an unwavering sense of professionalism and care. We set the bar high for our staff, because nothing matters more to us than giving children the summer of their lives.

Our camp mission is to provide a safe, fun and uplifting summer day camp experience for Jewish children; a place where love for Judaism is taught through fun, joy, and a true love for every staff member, camper and each member of their families. A few things we ask every potential staff member to consider before applying:

Dates

Camp season: Monday, June 28 – Friday, August 13, 2027
Specialty camp week (optional): June 21 – 25
Out-of-town staff: arrive in New Jersey by Thursday, June 24

Priority for staffing positions is given to people who can work the entire season. If you are interested in also joining the specialty camp week, please say so on your application. Applications for partial sessions are welcome, but full-season availability is preferred. Out-of-town staff who require accommodation need to commit to the full season (all seven weeks). On rare occasions an exception can be made if someone else we accept can take your position for the other part of the summer.

Timeliness

There are no vacation days during camp. Staff are expected to attend every single day of camp, on time. In case of illness, please notify the directors as soon as possible.

Pre-season training

Staff training is a requirement for employment, both by Camp Gan Israel and by our licensing bodies. It consists of online training, due at least two weeks before camp, and in-person staff training and camp setup: Girls Division and Kiddie Camp on Friday, June 25 and Sunday, June 27; Boys Division on Sunday, June 27. All staff are expected to be present.

Committed staff

Summer is a break from school for many, but working at camp is a serious undertaking: you are taking on the care of young children. It requires your energy, patience, excitement and commitment. By applying, you are ready to commit to giving it all you've got this summer.

Our commitment

We as a camp will do all we can to support you. Staff are always encouraged to bring any difficulty to our attention, and we will coach you and support you. We also show our appreciation for our staff's tireless work with fun nights out, trips and special surprise gifts. This summer will be rewarding for you, and you will see yourself grow from it.

Camp Gan Israel is an equal opportunity employer and does not discriminate on the basis of race, religion, sexual orientation or gender. Questions? office@ganisrael.org, or call, text or WhatsApp (862) 244-3420.

Our phone policy

Camp Gan Israel has a no-phone policy during camp hours. Each morning you leave your phone in the camp office and pick it up at the end of the day; staff stay in touch by radio. Phones pull our attention away from the campers, and campers feel it. If you have a reason to use your phone during camp, let your direct supervisor know and we'll be happy to accommodate.
